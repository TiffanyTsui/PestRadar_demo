Synthetic data only

Scenario reports, trap counts, phenology, biocontrol releases and weather variables on this page are synthetic, but structured like an operational greenhouse early-warning data model.

How to read this map

  • Greenhouse markers show modelled risk for each glasshouse under the chosen scenario and day.
  • The hotspot grid & kernel density mark where pressure is emerging, intensifying or persistent.
  • The forecast cone traces optimistic, expected and worst-case downwind spread.

Whitefly pressure starts in the Naaldwijk glasshouse cluster, then diffuses downwind. All sizes, colours and trails are illustrative, not measured.

Prototype assumptions

  • Westland greenhouse cluster near Naaldwijk.
  • Whitefly pressure shown over 3–14 days.
  • Diffusion uses weather, wind and greenhouse connectivity.
  • Hotspot classes show emerging, persistent and diminishing pressure.
