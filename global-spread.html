The pest

Phthorimaea absoluta
syn. Tuta absoluta
EN: tomato leaf miner · NL: tomatenmineermot · ES: cogollero del tomate / polilla del tomate
EPPO · GNORAB · A2 List

A South-American moth that devastates tomato crops. This map replays where and when EPPO first recorded it spreading across Europe, Africa and Asia.

How to read the map

  • Each dot is a country's first verified EPPO report, placed in that year.
  • Bigger, warmer dots = larger tomato exporters — a proxy for how big the problem is likely to be.
  • Soft trails trace the most likely introduction path into each new country.

Press play, or drag the year slider, to watch the invasion unfold. Size, colour and trails are illustrative, not measured.
